autopost1 ("we", "us") is a web application at masway07.github.io/autopost1 that helps you publish videos to your TikTok account. Contact: [EMAIL_ANDA].

2. Information we collect

When you connect TikTok, we receive your TikTok display name, avatar, and a unique account identifier (open_id), plus an access token that lets us post on your behalf. 3. How we use it

We use this information only to authenticate you, show which TikTok account is connected, and publish the content you submit to TikTok after you confirm. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Your content and token are shared only with TikTok as needed to publish your posts, and with service providers that host our infrastructure under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted. Uploaded videos are kept only as long as needed to complete publishing and are then deleted. We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can revoke autopost1's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services. You can also email us to request deletion of your data, and we will delete it within 30 days.
